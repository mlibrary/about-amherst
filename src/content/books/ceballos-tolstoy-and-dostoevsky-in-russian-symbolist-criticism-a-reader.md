description: At the turn of the twentieth century, Symbolist writers and
  thinkers at the forefront of Russian modernism cast Fyodor Dostoevsky and Leo
  Tolstoy as their chosen ancestors—examples to be exalted, repudiated, and
  ultimately surpassed. Their writings transformed the way these novelists were
  read within and beyond the Russian Empire. Many of the Symbolist writings on
  Tolstoy and Dostoevsky were long unavailable in English or were only available
  in outdated or unreliable translations. Presenting fresh annotated
  translations of some of the most influential Symbolist essays, Tolstoy and
  Dostoevsky in Russian Symbolist Criticism opens this globally influential body
  of criticism to a new anglophone audience.
