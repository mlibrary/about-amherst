ASEEES convenes over 2,800 scholars and practitioners of Slavic, East European, and Eurasian studies from around the world annually for more than 600 sessions and events.

2026 ASEEES Convention Theme: **States and Empires**

Chicago, November 12-15, 2026

<https://aseees.org/convention/2026-annual-convention/>

Join us to celebrate [ACP authors and titles in REEES](https://www.fulcrum.org/amherst?utf8=%E2%9C%93&press=amherst&q=russian)! 4-6pm, Saturday, November 14th for Books & Beverages, and a book giveaway raffle!

**ACP author Alexandra Perišić** will be recognized at ASEEES with an Honorable Mention for the University of Southern California Book Prize in Literary and Cultural Studies for *[Forgotten Friendships: Yugoslavia and the Anticolonial Francophone World](https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/rx913s71x?locale=en)*, which is also the [Association for Women in Slavic Studies' 2026 Heldt Prize winner](https://awsshome.org/awards/heldt-prizes/) for best book in New and Innovative Scholarship.
