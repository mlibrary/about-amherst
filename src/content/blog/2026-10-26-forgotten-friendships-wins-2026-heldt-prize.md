*[Forgotten Friendships: Yugoslavia and the Anticolonial Francophone World](https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/rx913s71x?locale=en)* was awarded the [Association for Women in Slavic Studies' 2026 Heldt Prize](https://awsshome.org/awards/heldt-prizes/) for **best book in New and Innovative Scholarship**, to be presented at the [2026 ASEEES Annual Convention in Chicago](https://aseees.org/convention/2026-annual-convention/).
